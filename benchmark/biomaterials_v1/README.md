# Biomaterials V1 deterministic closure (live gate pending)

This is the offline-first deterministic benchmark for the tenth and final planned Synthex scientific domain. It is not frozen: the live-provider gate remains unsatisfied. Eight locked official publisher PDFs (one cross-domain negative reuses the existing Membranes source) and eight independently frozen gold files are listed in `source_locks.json`. `offline_recovery.py` reads only locked PDF text, not gold. `build_archive.py` creates distinct material, fabrication process, specimen, experiment, calculation, and measurement records; the generic archive's `DeviceEntity` represents a scaffold, hydrogel, or filament specimen, not an electronic device. `biomaterials_contract.py` gates units, raw numbers, source evidence, specimen links, biological context, and computational-only endpoints. `analytics.py` retains specimen and assay identity rather than averaging it away; calculations do not enter experimental analytics.

Total: 158 exact outputs (126 experimental, 32 computational), 94 experiments, and 16 calculations. The recovery parsers are table/passage-scoped and fail closed when their source layouts change; no DOI/title special case supplies values. The source/gold hashes prevent silent substitution. The new papers were visually spot-checked at principal table/result pages. The locked results are not a complete extraction of every property in these papers.

The Table 1 FDM settings in D are recovered as six process parameters for each of the two printed-scaffold specimens (12 parameter records), separate from the 21 result outputs. Table-result evidence retains a table ID and sample/property locator; source numeric strings retain publisher precision. Reported uncertainty magnitudes are not yet included in the 101-point scoring gold and should be added only through a new explicitly versioned gold lock.

Scientific boundaries: cell viability, cytotoxicity, metabolic activity, proliferation, hemolysis, antimicrobial inhibition zone, bacterial viability, mass loss, degradation rate, swelling ratio, water uptake, porosity, pore size, strut/fiber diameter, surface properties, viscosity, and mechanical properties are separate keys. Biological results retain cell line or organism/strain, assay, specimen and time where reported. An unreported time remains absent. Drug-delivery kinetics are outside V1 scope. Predicted energies belong only to calculations, not experimental assay records. Review and negative-route controls have zero canonical Biomaterials outputs.

`python -m benchmark.biomaterials_v1.closure` verifies eight source/gold locks, exact recovered/canonical points, 40 scientific invariants, record/evidence/analytics/graph contamination, and persisted provider history. It writes `closure_audit.json` and `draft_freeze_manifest.json`. The draft manifest is `pending_live_gate`: deterministic readiness is not a freeze authorization. B is antimicrobial rather than osteogenic because its official primary table provides exact strain-specific results. C has two exact time endpoints rather than a dense tabulated time series. H does not have an exact focal degradation/swelling endpoint in the selected passages, so none is inferred.

`discover_gemini.py` performs a source-free model listing only. `prepare_live.py` checks gold/PDF hashes and computes hashes of the exact source-only extraction prompts locally; it does not instantiate a provider client or transmit. `live_preparation.json` describes that local preparation, not the authorization or status of later runs. `run_live.py` is restricted to A/B and three discovered exact Gemini IDs, verifies the same locked prompt hashes, caps attempts at three per model across A/B (counting interrupted attempt artifacts), disables model fallback, and uses immutable raw-response artifacts before validation. In the authorized live gate, BIO-GOLD-A source text was sent to all three exact IDs: the 3.8 and 3.5 models each returned three 503 unavailable responses, and the 2.5 model returned one non-transient 404 unavailable-to-new-users response. No payload was returned, so no raw-response body, scientific score, validation, or canonical admission exists. BIO-BIOACT-B was not sent because the newer models exhausted their attempt budgets and the remaining model was unavailable. `score_persisted_raw.py` independently verifies an immutable raw-response hash and conservatively scores exact specimen/property/value/unit and biological context against frozen A/B gold when a future run returns a payload; it reports page/table evidence and focal ownership separately from canonical admission. A later scientific audit must also inspect cell-line/time/assay collapse, viability–cytotoxicity inversion, swelling–mass-loss confusion, organism swaps, invented controls, process leakage, and filled missing cells.
